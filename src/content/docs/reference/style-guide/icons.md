When publications are live, they are listed on Remote, the web application for controlling and configuring Little Printer. Each publication is listed with a title, brief description and an icon.

Your icon should be:

- Black and white
- 55 x 55 pixels
- Saved as a PNG
- Hosted on your publication at `/icon.png`
